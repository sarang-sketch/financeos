# Requirements Document

## Introduction

FinanceOS is an AI Financial Control Tower for Indian businesses, built as an intelligence and control layer on top of Razorpay payment data, Indian financial rules, and an internal semantic ledger. Razorpay moves the money. Supabase stores the financial state. The Semantic Ledger understands the accounting state. AI explains what is happening. Agents investigate problems. The Policy Engine controls what AI is allowed to do. The Audit Log proves what happened.

FinanceOS exposes exactly four Agent identities throughout the product contract: Finance Analyst, Reconciliation Agent, India Compliance Agent, and Cash Agent. The Reconciliation Agent owns both the full payment-to-ledger lifecycle and Razorpay Route seller payout reconciliation. The Finance Analyst owns both period-over-period financial explanation and failed-payment recovery intelligence. The India Compliance Agent owns India-native detection and review. The Cash Agent owns forecasting, affordability, and post-correction cash impact.

The centerpiece capability is continuous reconciliation of the Razorpay lifecycle: payment → Razorpay fee → GST on fee → settlement → bank → ledger. Around that centerpiece, FinanceOS detects India-specific financial exceptions, explains each exception with traceable evidence, forecasts cash impact, and proposes or executes corrective actions under a policy, approval, and audit regime.

Every AI-surfaced figure is tool-grounded: it is derived from a stored source record and is traceable back to that record. Actions follow a fixed pipeline: DETECT → INVESTIGATE → EXPLAIN → PROPOSE → AUTHORIZE → EXECUTE → VERIFY.

The winning demo is deliberately one story: investigate a Tool_Grounded ₹3.82 lakh settlement shortfall, present evidence-backed safe corrections, obtain human approval where policy requires approval, execute and verify the correction, and show the verified cash impact. The winning demo uses Razorpay test-mode data and action endpoints only.

## Non-Goals

The following are explicitly outside the scope of this feature. Requirements in this document MUST NOT be interpreted as requiring them.

- **Not an ERP**: FinanceOS does not implement procurement, inventory, manufacturing, HR, or general business process management. It reads and reconciles financial state; it does not become the system of record for business operations.
- **Not a tax filing or compliance authority**: The India Compliance Agent performs detection and review only. FinanceOS does not file GST returns, does not file TDS returns, does not generate statutory filings, and does not provide authoritative tax advice.
- **Not a Razorpay replacement**: FinanceOS does not process payments, hold funds, or move money on its own rails. Money movement remains with Razorpay. FinanceOS reads Razorpay data and, where authorized, invokes Razorpay APIs.
- **Not a live-mode Razorpay release**: The MVP and winning demo use Razorpay test-mode credentials, records, and endpoints. Promotion to Razorpay live mode is outside this feature contract.
- **Not an open-ended Agent catalogue**: FinanceOS exposes no separate Agent identity for marketplace seller payout reconciliation or failed-payment recovery and exposes no fifth Agent identity. Marketplace seller payout reconciliation remains a Reconciliation Agent capability, and failed-payment recovery remains a Finance Analyst capability.
- **Not a multi-scenario demo tour**: The winning demo does not replace the ₹3.82 lakh settlement investigation with a separate marketplace, recovery, compliance, or generic chatbot scenario.
- **Not a bookkeeping replacement for statutory books**: The Semantic Ledger represents accounting state for reconciliation and explanation purposes, not certified statutory books of account.
- **Not an autonomous money mover**: No Sensitive_Action executes without recorded human Authorization.

## Glossary

### Indian Finance Terms

- **GST**: Goods and Services Tax, the indirect tax levied on the supply of goods and services in India.
- **GST_On_Fee**: The GST component charged by Razorpay on its own processing fee for a Payment.
- **ITC**: Input Tax Credit, the credit a business claims for GST paid on inward supplies.
- **ITC_Discrepancy**: A difference between the ITC amount implied by stored Invoice and Payment records and the ITC amount recorded in the Semantic Ledger.
- **TDS**: Tax Deducted at Source, tax withheld by a payer on specified payments.
- **TDS_Review_Item**: A stored transaction that FinanceOS flags as potentially requiring TDS deduction or TDS rate review.
- **GSTIN**: Goods and Services Tax Identification Number, the 15-character registration identifier of a GST-registered entity.
- **HSN_SAC**: Harmonised System of Nomenclature code for goods, or Services Accounting Code for services, recorded on an Invoice line.
- **Credit_Note**: A document reducing the value of a previously issued Invoice.
- **Unmatched_Credit_Note**: A Credit_Note with no linked Invoice, or with a linked Invoice whose adjusted value does not reconcile to the Credit_Note value.
- **Lakh**: Indian numbering unit equal to 100,000.
- **Crore**: Indian numbering unit equal to 10,000,000.
- **Indian_Number_Format**: Digit grouping of 2,2,3 from the right with the Indian Rupee symbol, for example ₹3,82,000.
- **INR**: Indian Rupee, the single currency of all monetary values in FinanceOS.
- **Payroll_Obligation**: A recorded recurring outflow representing employee compensation due on a specified date.

### Razorpay Object Terms

- **Payment**: A Razorpay payment object representing a customer payment attempt and its outcome.
- **Order**: A Razorpay order object representing an intended collection against which Payments are made.
- **Refund**: A Razorpay refund object representing the return of funds for a Payment.
- **Settlement**: A Razorpay settlement object representing a batched transfer of funds to the merchant bank account.
- **Settlement_Recon_Report**: The Razorpay settlement reconciliation report enumerating the individual Payments, Refunds, fees, and taxes composing a Settlement.
- **Razorpay_Fee**: The processing fee Razorpay deducts for a Payment.
- **Razorpay_Route**: The Razorpay feature that splits a single customer Payment across one or more Linked_Accounts and the platform.
- **Linked_Account**: A Razorpay Route sub-merchant account, representing a Seller in a marketplace.
- **Transfer**: A Razorpay Route object moving a portion of a Payment to a Linked_Account.
- **Transfer_Reversal**: A Razorpay Route object reversing all or part of a Transfer.
- **Razorpay_Invoice**: A Razorpay invoice object ingested as a source record.
- **Failed_Payment**: A Payment whose Razorpay status indicates the collection did not succeed.

### FinanceOS System Terms

- **Control_Tower**: The FinanceOS dashboard surface presenting metrics and the Attention_Panel.
- **Attention_Panel**: The Control_Tower region listing Exception_Categories with an item count and an aggregate INR value.
- **Exception**: A detected financial anomaly with a category, an INR impact value, and links to its Source_Records.
- **Exception_Category**: A named grouping of Exceptions. The categories used in this document are: settlement mismatch, possible duplicate Refund, unmatched credit note, missing accrual, ambiguous match, GST anomaly, missing GST information, invalid GSTIN, ITC discrepancy, record needing review, seller settlement mismatch, over-allocated split, verification failure, and execution failure.
- **Semantic_Ledger**: The internal double-entry-aware ledger representing the accounting state of a Tenant.
- **Ledger_Entry**: A single line in the Semantic_Ledger with an account, a debit or credit amount in INR, and a link to its Source_Records.
- **Source_Record**: A stored ingested or derived record, identified by type and identifier, from which a figure is computed.
- **Evidence_Chain**: The ordered set of Source_Records and computation steps supporting a stated figure or conclusion.
- **Tool_Grounded**: A property of a figure meaning the figure was produced by a Financial_Tool over stored records, not generated by a Model.
- **Financial_Tool**: A typed, server-side function that an Agent invokes to read or compute over Tenant data.
- **Financial_Tool_Layer**: The complete set of Financial_Tools exposed to Agents.
- **Agent**: An AI-driven component that executes the Action_Pipeline for a defined domain and whose identity belongs to the Agent_Catalogue.
- **Agent_Catalogue**: The closed product-wide set containing exactly Finance_Analyst, Reconciliation_Agent, India_Compliance_Agent, and Cash_Agent.
- **Finance_Analyst**: The Agent exposed as “Finance Analyst” that explains period-over-period financial change, analyses Failed_Payments, computes recovery probability, and proposes policy-gated recovery actions.
- **Reconciliation_Agent**: The Agent exposed as “Reconciliation Agent” that reconciles Payment → Razorpay_Invoice → Settlement → Semantic_Ledger, reconciles Razorpay_Route seller payout flows for Linked_Accounts, and reports discrepancies.
- **India_Compliance_Agent**: The Agent exposed as “India Compliance Agent” that performs India-native tax detection and review over stored records without producing a filing or authoritative tax position.
- **Cash_Agent**: The Agent exposed as “Cash Agent” that produces Cash_Forecasts, answers affordability questions, and explains the verified cash impact of corrective actions.
- **Winning_Demo**: The single primary product demonstration that follows the Demo_Settlement_Investigation through evidence, policy-gated correction or approval, execution, Verification, and cash impact.
- **Demo_Settlement_Investigation**: The Tool_Grounded investigation of an aggregate settlement shortfall of 38200000 paise, displayed as ₹3,82,000.00 or ₹3.82 lakh, using Razorpay test-mode records.
- **Action_Pipeline**: The fixed sequence DETECT → INVESTIGATE → EXPLAIN → PROPOSE → AUTHORIZE → EXECUTE → VERIFY.
- **Proposal**: A structured corrective action produced by an Agent, containing an action type, target records, INR impact, and an Evidence_Chain.
- **Policy_Engine**: The component that evaluates a Proposal against Policy_Checks and returns an authorization decision.
- **Policy_Check**: One of the gate evaluations: user permission, accounting rule, transaction evidence, duplicate action, risk threshold, and approval requirement.
- **Safe_Action**: A Proposal that passes all Policy_Checks and whose risk score is at or below the Auto_Execute_Threshold.
- **Sensitive_Action**: A Proposal that requires recorded human Authorization before execution.
- **Auto_Execute_Threshold**: The Tenant-configured risk score boundary at or below which a Proposal is a Safe_Action.
- **Authorization**: A recorded decision by a User or by the Policy_Engine permitting execution of a Proposal.
- **Verification**: The post-execution check comparing observed state against the Proposal's expected outcome.
- **Audit_Event**: An immutable append-only record of one Action_Pipeline stage occurrence.
- **Audit_Log**: The append-only collection of Audit_Events for a Tenant.
- **AI_Gateway**: The component that routes Model requests across Model_Providers with fallback, cost, latency, and secret-safe diagnostics awareness.
- **Model_Provider**: An external large language model service, one of OpenRouter, Gemini, or Groq. OpenRouter is itself a gateway that proxies frontier reasoning models, so the resolved underlying model name is recorded alongside the provider name.
- **Model**: A specific large language model offered by a Model_Provider.
- **Server_Runtime_Environment**: A process environment available only to server-side code and excluded from client bundles.
- **Encrypted_Secret_Storage**: Server-side storage that retains a credential value in encrypted form and releases the credential value only to an authorized server process.
- **Masked_Credential_Reference**: A non-secret reference that identifies the Model_Provider and whether a credential is configured without containing any credential characters beyond a fixed redaction marker.
- **Non_Financial_Probe**: A fixed provider smoke-check request containing zero Tenant identifiers, zero Source_Record fields, zero monetary figures, zero Tenant-derived text, and zero credential values in its request content.
- **Provider_Readiness_Check**: A server-side check of one Model_Provider that resolves credential state, sends a Non_Financial_Probe where a credential is available, and produces a Provider_Readiness_Result.
- **Provider_Diagnostic_Outcome**: Exactly one of ready, missing credential, invalid credential, timeout, rate limit, or provider failure.
- **Provider_Readiness_Result**: An operator-readable record containing the Model_Provider name, resolved Model name or an unavailable marker, elapsed latency in milliseconds, Provider_Diagnostic_Outcome, redacted diagnostic code, and check timestamp.
- **Routing_Verification**: A non-financial diagnostic run that records the actual first Model_Provider attempt and the actual fallback attempt for a declared Task_Class under controlled failure outcomes.
- **Cash_Forecast**: A dated projection of Tenant cash position over a forecast horizon.
- **Simulation**: A Cash_Forecast or Proposal outcome computed without writing changes to Tenant state.
- **Tenant**: An isolated customer organisation whose data is separated by row-level security.
- **User**: An authenticated person acting within exactly one Tenant per session.
- **Seller**: The business entity represented by a Linked_Account.
- **Runway**: The number of months of Tenant operation supported by current cash at the current net monthly burn.
- **Minimum_Sample_Size**: The Tenant-configured minimum count of historical Payments required before a recovery probability supports a Proposal.
- **Approval_Window**: The Tenant-configured period, from 1 hour to 168 hours and defaulting to 24 hours, within which a Sensitive_Action must be approved or rejected before the Proposal expires.
- **Chain_Value**: The tamper-evidence value stored on an Audit_Event, computed over that Audit_Event's stored fields together with the Chain_Value of the preceding Audit_Event.
- **Headroom**: Projected closing cash minus the obligation amount.
- **Ingestion_Run**: One execution of the FinanceOS_Ingestion_Service for a Tenant, recording a start timestamp, an end timestamp, and a run status.
- **Lookback_Window**: The Tenant-configured historical period, from 30 days to 730 days and defaulting to 180 days, over which channel success rates are computed.
- **Maximum_Retry_Age**: The Tenant-configured age limit, from 1 day to 30 days and defaulting to 7 days, beyond which no retry Proposal is created for a Failed_Payment.
- **Forecast_Horizon**: The Tenant-configured forecast length, from 30 days to 180 days and defaulting to 90 days.
- **Safety_Buffer**: The Tenant-configured cash cushion, from 0 paise to ₹10 Crore, defaulting to 10 percent of the obligation amount.
- **Unusual_Multiple**: The Tenant-configured multiple of the median transaction amount, from 1.5 to 20.0 and defaulting to 5.0, at or above which a transaction is classified as unusual.
- **Task_Class**: One of complex reasoning, document analysis, or fast classification, declared by an Agent on a Model request.
- **Permission**: One of view financial data, run agents, approve sensitive actions, configure policy, manage credentials, or manage users.

### FinanceOS Component Names

- **FinanceOS_Ingestion_Service**: The component that retrieves and stores Razorpay objects for a Tenant.
- **FinanceOS_Audit_Service**: The component that appends and serves Audit_Events.
- **FinanceOS_Action_Service**: The component that executes authorized Proposals and performs Verification.
- **FinanceOS_Calculation_Service**: The component that performs monetary arithmetic and rounding.
- **FinanceOS_Response_Validator**: The component that checks Agent responses for Tool_Grounded figures before display.
- **FinanceOS_Data_Layer**: The Supabase Postgres storage layer enforcing row-level security.
- **FinanceOS_API**: The authenticated server interface through which clients read and write Tenant data.
- **FinanceOS_Configuration_Service**: The component that stores Tenant configuration and supplies credentials only from the Server_Runtime_Environment or Encrypted_Secret_Storage.
- **Provider_Runtime_Verifier**: The server-only component that performs Provider_Readiness_Checks and Routing_Verifications without sending Tenant financial data.
- **Secret_Safety_Gate**: The component that prevents Model_Provider credential values from entering source files, client bundles, logs, Model prompts, error messages, test fixtures, or Audit_Event payloads.
- **FinanceOS_Authorization_Service**: The component that evaluates User permissions within a Tenant.
- **FinanceOS_UI**: The client interface presenting the Control_Tower, Agent conversations, and Evidence_Chains.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Razorpay Data Ingestion

**User Story:** As a finance operator, I want FinanceOS to ingest my Razorpay data, so that every reconciliation and explanation is based on actual source records rather than estimates.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a User initiates ingestion for a Tenant, THE FinanceOS_Ingestion_Service SHALL create an ingestion run recording the Tenant identifier, the run start timestamp, and a run status of in progress, SHALL retrieve Payments, Refunds, Settlements, Settlement_Recon_Reports, Transfers, Transfer_Reversals, Orders, Razorpay_Invoices, and Linked_Accounts from the Razorpay test-mode API in successive pages of 100 records per object type, SHALL apply a timeout of 30 seconds to each Razorpay API request, and SHALL stop requesting further pages for an object type when a response returns fewer than 100 records.
2. WHEN THE FinanceOS_Ingestion_Service stores a retrieved Razorpay object, THE FinanceOS_Ingestion_Service SHALL store the Razorpay object identifier, the object type, the Tenant identifier, the ingestion run identifier, the retrieval timestamp, and the unmodified payload.
3. WHEN THE FinanceOS_Ingestion_Service retrieves a Razorpay object whose identifier already exists for the Tenant, THE FinanceOS_Ingestion_Service SHALL replace the stored payload with the newly retrieved payload, SHALL update the retrieval timestamp, and SHALL retain a single record per Razorpay object identifier per Tenant.
4. IF the Razorpay API returns an error response other than a rate limit response and other than a credential rejection, THEN THE FinanceOS_Ingestion_Service SHALL record the error code, the requested object type, the request timestamp, and the ingestion run identifier, and SHALL continue ingesting the remaining object types.
5. IF the Razorpay API returns a rate limit response or a Razorpay API request exceeds the 30 second timeout, THEN THE FinanceOS_Ingestion_Service SHALL retry that request after an initial delay of 1 second, SHALL double the delay before each subsequent retry up to a maximum delay of 16 seconds, SHALL perform at most 5 retries for that request, and SHALL record the request as an error for its object type when the 5th retry does not return a success response.
6. WHEN ingestion for a Tenant completes, THE FinanceOS_Ingestion_Service SHALL record the ingestion run end timestamp, a per-object-type count of records stored, a per-object-type count of errors encountered, and a run status of completed when zero errors were encountered, partially completed when 1 or more errors were encountered and 1 or more records were stored, or failed when zero records were stored.
7. WHEN THE FinanceOS_Ingestion_Service stores a monetary value from a Razorpay object, THE FinanceOS_Ingestion_Service SHALL store the value as an integer number of paise within the inclusive range 0 to 999,999,999,999, SHALL apply no rounding, truncation, or unit scaling to the retrieved value, and SHALL record the currency as INR.
8. WHERE no ingestion run with a run status of completed or partially completed exists for the Tenant, THE FinanceOS_Ingestion_Service SHALL restrict retrieval to Razorpay objects created within the 365 days immediately preceding the ingestion run start timestamp.
9. WHERE at least 1 ingestion run with a run status of completed exists for the Tenant, THE FinanceOS_Ingestion_Service SHALL restrict retrieval to Razorpay objects created at or after the run start timestamp of the most recent ingestion run with a run status of completed.
10. IF the Razorpay API rejects a request because the Tenant's Razorpay API credential is invalid or expired, THEN THE FinanceOS_Ingestion_Service SHALL stop the ingestion run without requesting further object types, SHALL set the run status to failed with an indication that the credential was rejected, SHALL store no Razorpay objects for that run, and SHALL leave Razorpay objects previously stored for the Tenant unchanged.

### Requirement 2: Semantic Ledger

**User Story:** As a finance operator, I want an internal double-entry-aware ledger derived from my payment data, so that FinanceOS can compare accounting state against payment state.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN THE Semantic_Ledger records a financial event derived from a stored Payment, Refund, Settlement, Transfer, or Transfer_Reversal Source_Record, THE Semantic_Ledger SHALL create one Ledger_Entry set containing at least 2 and at most 20 Ledger_Entries, SHALL assign each Ledger_Entry exactly 1 account from the Tenant chart of accounts and exactly 1 amount that is an integer number of paise greater than 0 designated as either debit or credit, and SHALL create the set only when its total debit amount equals its total credit amount.
2. WHEN THE Semantic_Ledger creates a Ledger_Entry, THE Semantic_Ledger SHALL store at least 1 Source_Record link for that Ledger_Entry, and SHALL store for each link the Source_Record type and the Source_Record identifier from which the Ledger_Entry amount was derived.
3. WHEN THE Semantic_Ledger records a Payment, THE Semantic_Ledger SHALL create separate Ledger_Entries for the gross Payment amount, the Razorpay_Fee, and the GST_On_Fee, each as an integer number of paise read from the stored Payment Source_Record, and SHALL post to the settlement-pending account in the Tenant chart of accounts an amount equal to the gross Payment amount minus the Razorpay_Fee minus the GST_On_Fee with a difference of 0 paise.
4. WHEN a User requests correction of a persisted Ledger_Entry, THE Semantic_Ledger SHALL create a new Ledger_Entry set whose per-account amounts equal the original set's per-account amounts with the debit and credit designations exchanged, SHALL store on the new set the identifier of the original Ledger_Entry set, and SHALL retain every original Ledger_Entry with its account, amount, debit or credit designation, and Source_Record links unchanged.
5. WHEN a User requests a trial balance for a date range whose start date is on or before its end date, THE Semantic_Ledger SHALL return, for each account in the Tenant chart of accounts holding 1 or more Ledger_Entries dated within the inclusive range, the total debit amount, the total credit amount, and the closing balance, each as an integer number of paise in INR, and SHALL return a result whose summed debit total equals its summed credit total.
6. IF a requested Semantic_Ledger write would produce unequal total debits and total credits, THEN THE Semantic_Ledger SHALL reject the entire Ledger_Entry set, SHALL persist 0 Ledger_Entries from that set, SHALL record the rejection reason including the imbalance amount in paise and the Source_Record identifiers involved, and SHALL return an error response indicating that the set is unbalanced.
7. THE Semantic_Ledger SHALL reject any request to modify or delete a persisted Ledger_Entry, and SHALL maintain for every persisted Ledger_Entry set a sum of debit amounts minus sum of credit amounts equal to 0 paise.
8. WHEN THE Semantic_Ledger derives Ledger_Entries from a Source_Record for which a Ledger_Entry set already exists for the Tenant, THE Semantic_Ledger SHALL retain the existing Ledger_Entry set, SHALL create 0 additional Ledger_Entries, and SHALL leave every account balance unchanged.
9. WHEN THE Semantic_Ledger records a Refund, THE Semantic_Ledger SHALL create a Ledger_Entry set whose debit and credit designations are opposite to those used for the refunded Payment, SHALL set the total amount of that set equal to the stored Refund amount in integer paise, and SHALL store the Refund identifier and the refunded Payment identifier as Source_Record links.
10. WHEN THE Semantic_Ledger records a Settlement, THE Semantic_Ledger SHALL create a Ledger_Entry set that reduces the settlement-pending account and increases the bank account by the Settlement received amount in integer paise, and SHALL store the Settlement identifier and the Settlement_Recon_Report identifier as Source_Record links.

### Requirement 3: Control Tower Dashboard

**User Story:** As a business owner, I want a single dashboard showing my financial position and what needs attention, so that I can see my cash reality and open exceptions in one place.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a User opens THE Control_Tower, THE Control_Tower SHALL display within 3 seconds the Cash metric as the Tenant's Settlement received amounts minus the Tenant's recorded outflows as of the current date, the Revenue metric as captured Payment amounts minus Refund amounts over the 30 calendar days ending on the current date, the Pending Settlement metric as the captured Payment amounts not yet linked to a Settlement, and the Runway metric, each computed only from Source_Records whose Tenant identifier equals the User's Tenant identifier.
2. WHEN THE Control_Tower displays a monetary metric, THE Control_Tower SHALL render the value in INR using Indian_Number_Format.
3. IF a monetary value displayed by THE Control_Tower is at or above 100000 AND below 10000000, THEN THE Control_Tower SHALL additionally display the value in Lakh units to 2 decimal places.
4. WHEN THE Control_Tower displays a Runway metric value from 0.0 to 120.0 months, THE Control_Tower SHALL express the value in months to 1 decimal place.
5. WHEN THE Control_Tower renders the Attention_Panel, THE Control_Tower SHALL display one row per Exception_Category having 1 or more open Exceptions containing the category name, the count of open Exceptions, and the aggregate INR impact of those Exceptions, SHALL order the rows by descending aggregate INR impact, and SHALL order rows of equal aggregate INR impact by ascending alphabetical category name.
6. WHEN a User selects an Attention_Panel row by pointer or by keyboard, THE Control_Tower SHALL display the open Exceptions in that Exception_Category ordered by descending INR impact with equal-impact Exceptions ordered by ascending Exception identifier, in pages of at most 50 Exceptions, each showing the Exception's INR impact, the Exception's Source_Record identifiers, and a control that opens the Exception's Evidence_Chain.
7. WHILE a Tenant has zero ingested Razorpay objects, THE Control_Tower SHALL display an empty-state message identifying that ingestion has not completed, SHALL display no monetary metric values, and SHALL keep navigation and interface controls operable.
8. WHILE a Tenant has 1 or more ingested Razorpay objects AND metric computation for the Tenant has been incomplete for 30 seconds or less since that computation started, THE Control_Tower SHALL display a processing state for each incomplete metric and SHALL keep navigation and interface controls operable.
9. IF metric computation for a metric returns an error, OR IF metric computation for a metric does not complete within 30 seconds of starting, THEN THE Control_Tower SHALL display a failure state for that metric identifying the metric name and identifying whether the cause was a computation error or a timeout, SHALL display a retry control for that metric, and SHALL display the remaining metrics that computed successfully.
10. WHEN THE Control_Tower displays a metric value, THE Control_Tower SHALL display the timestamp of the most recent ingestion that contributed to the value in Indian Standard Time to whole-second precision.
11. IF a monetary value displayed by THE Control_Tower is at or above 10000000, THEN THE Control_Tower SHALL additionally display the value in Crore units to 2 decimal places.
12. IF the Runway value for the User's Tenant exceeds 120.0 months, OR IF THE Cash_Agent reports Runway as not applicable, THEN THE Control_Tower SHALL display a non-numeric Runway state identifying which of those two conditions applies and SHALL display no Runway number.
13. IF a Tenant has zero open Exceptions across all Exception_Categories, THEN THE Control_Tower SHALL display a no-open-exceptions state in the Attention_Panel and SHALL display no Attention_Panel rows.

### Requirement 4: Reconciliation Agent

**User Story:** As a finance operator, I want an agent that reconciles the full Razorpay payment-to-ledger lifecycle, so that I can find and explain every rupee of settlement difference.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN THE Reconciliation_Agent runs for a Tenant, THE Reconciliation_Agent SHALL match each Payment in the run scope to its Order, its Razorpay_Invoice, its Settlement, and its Ledger_Entries using only the stored Razorpay object identifier links on those records, SHALL perform no amount-based or date-based inferred matching, and SHALL record per Payment the matched identifier for each of the 4 record types or a not-matched marker for each record type with no linked record.
2. WHEN THE Reconciliation_Agent evaluates a Settlement, THE Reconciliation_Agent SHALL compute the Expected Amount in integer paise as the sum of Payment amounts enumerated in the Settlement_Recon_Report minus the sum of Refund amounts enumerated in that report minus the sum of chargeback amounts enumerated in that report plus the signed sum of adjustment amounts enumerated in that report, and SHALL compute the Difference in integer paise as the Expected Amount minus the Settlement received amount.
3. WHEN THE Reconciliation_Agent computes a Difference for a Settlement that is not equal to 0 paise, THE Reconciliation_Agent SHALL decompose the Difference in integer paise into a Razorpay_Fee component equal to the sum of Razorpay_Fee amounts enumerated in the Settlement_Recon_Report, a GST_On_Fee component equal to the sum of GST_On_Fee amounts enumerated in that report, and an unexplained residual component equal to the Difference minus the Razorpay_Fee component minus the GST_On_Fee component.
4. WHEN the unexplained residual component of a Settlement Difference equals 0 paise, THE Reconciliation_Agent SHALL mark the Settlement as "difference explained" and SHALL record, against the Settlement identifier, the Expected Amount, the received amount, the Difference, the Razorpay_Fee component, and the GST_On_Fee component.
5. WHEN the unexplained residual component of a Settlement Difference is not equal to 0 paise, THE Reconciliation_Agent SHALL create an Exception in the settlement mismatch Exception_Category with the absolute value of the residual as the INR impact, SHALL classify the residual as an unexplained shortfall where the residual is greater than 0 paise and as an unexplained excess where the residual is less than 0 paise, and SHALL reference the Settlement identifier and the Settlement_Recon_Report identifier.
6. WHEN a User asks THE Reconciliation_Agent about a settlement shortfall, THE Reconciliation_Agent SHALL return one breakdown row per in-scope Settlement whose Difference is not equal to 0 paise, ordered by descending absolute Difference, each row containing the Settlement identifier, the Expected Amount, the received amount, the Difference, the Razorpay_Fee component, the GST_On_Fee component, and the unexplained residual component, and SHALL limit the returned rows to the 50 Settlements with the largest absolute Difference plus a single aggregate row stating the count and the total absolute Difference of the remaining in-scope Settlements.
7. WHEN THE Reconciliation_Agent reports a total shortfall figure, THE Reconciliation_Agent SHALL report the settlement date range applied to that figure, applying the trailing 90 days ending at the run timestamp where the request states no date range, and SHALL report the count of Payments, Settlements, Refunds, Ledger_Entries, and Razorpay_Invoices examined and the count of Settlements with a non-zero unexplained residual component.
8. WHEN THE Reconciliation_Agent detects 2 or more Refunds against the same Payment whose combined amount exceeds the Payment amount by 1 paisa or more, THE Reconciliation_Agent SHALL create an Exception in the possible duplicate Refund Exception_Category with the amount by which the combined Refund amount exceeds the Payment amount as the INR impact, SHALL reference the Payment identifier and each contributing Refund identifier, and SHALL create no Exception in this Exception_Category where the combined Refund amount is at or below the Payment amount.
9. WHEN THE Reconciliation_Agent detects a Credit_Note that is an Unmatched_Credit_Note, THE Reconciliation_Agent SHALL create an Exception in the unmatched credit note Exception_Category with the Credit_Note value as the INR impact and SHALL reference the Credit_Note identifier and, where a linked Invoice exists, the linked Invoice identifier.
10. WHEN THE Reconciliation_Agent detects a Payment or a Refund with no Ledger_Entry referencing that Payment or that Refund as a Source_Record, THE Reconciliation_Agent SHALL create an Exception in the missing accrual Exception_Category with the Payment amount or the Refund amount as the INR impact and SHALL reference that Payment or Refund identifier.
11. IF THE Reconciliation_Agent cannot match a Payment to any Settlement, THEN THE Reconciliation_Agent SHALL classify the Payment as unsettled, SHALL record the Payment identifier and the Payment age in whole days from the Payment creation timestamp to the run timestamp, and SHALL exclude the Payment from every Settlement Expected Amount and Difference computation in that run.
12. FOR ALL Exceptions created by THE Reconciliation_Agent, the Exception SHALL reference at least 1 Source_Record identifier, the Exception INR impact SHALL be stored as an integer number of paise, and the Exception lifecycle state at creation SHALL be open.
13. IF the Settlement_Recon_Report for a Settlement is absent or enumerates 0 Payments, THEN THE Reconciliation_Agent SHALL mark the Settlement as unreconciled, SHALL compute no Expected Amount and no Difference for that Settlement, SHALL exclude the Settlement from the reported total shortfall figure, and SHALL report the Settlement identifier together with the absent or empty source record type.
14. ### Requirement 5: Action Pipeline and Policy Engine

**User Story:** As a business owner, I want every AI-initiated action to pass a policy gate with approval and audit, so that AI can act on my finances without me losing control.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN an Agent creates a Proposal, THE Agent SHALL execute the Action_Pipeline stages in the order DETECT, INVESTIGATE, EXPLAIN, PROPOSE, AUTHORIZE, EXECUTE, VERIFY, SHALL complete each stage before beginning the next stage, and SHALL omit no stage.
2. WHEN an Agent completes an Action_Pipeline stage, THE FinanceOS_Audit_Service SHALL append, within 5 seconds of stage completion, an Audit_Event recording the stage name, the Agent identifier, the Tenant identifier, the Proposal identifier, the timestamp, and the stage outcome as exactly one of succeeded, failed, or blocked.
3. WHEN an Agent submits a Proposal to THE Policy_Engine, THE Policy_Engine SHALL evaluate all 6 Policy_Checks, namely the user permission Policy_Check, the accounting rule Policy_Check, the transaction evidence Policy_Check, the duplicate action Policy_Check, the risk threshold Policy_Check, and the approval requirement Policy_Check, SHALL evaluate each Policy_Check independently of the result of every other Policy_Check, and SHALL return the evaluation within 10 seconds of submission.
4. WHEN THE Policy_Engine completes evaluation of a Proposal, THE Policy_Engine SHALL return a pass or fail result for each of the 6 Policy_Checks, the computed Proposal risk score, the Auto_Execute_Threshold value used, and exactly one decision of auto-execute, require-approval, or block.
5. IF 1 or more Policy_Checks return fail, THEN THE Policy_Engine SHALL return the block decision, SHALL record the identifier of each failed Policy_Check, SHALL retain the Proposal without execution, and SHALL make no change to Tenant state.
6. WHEN all 6 Policy_Checks pass AND the Proposal risk score is at or below the Auto_Execute_Threshold, THE Policy_Engine SHALL classify the Proposal as a Safe_Action, SHALL return the auto-execute decision, and SHALL record an Authorization identifying THE Policy_Engine as the authorizing actor.
7. WHEN all 6 Policy_Checks pass AND the Proposal risk score exceeds the Auto_Execute_Threshold, THE Policy_Engine SHALL classify the Proposal as a Sensitive_Action and SHALL return the require-approval decision.
8. WHILE a Sensitive_Action holds the require-approval decision, THE FinanceOS_Action_Service SHALL withhold execution of the Sensitive_Action and SHALL make no change to Tenant state for that Proposal.
9. WHEN a User approves a Sensitive_Action, THE FinanceOS_Action_Service SHALL record an Authorization containing the User identifier, the Proposal identifier, and the decision timestamp, SHALL resubmit the Proposal to THE Policy_Engine, and SHALL execute the Proposal only where the resubmitted evaluation returns a decision other than block.
10. WHEN a User rejects a Sensitive_Action, THE FinanceOS_Action_Service SHALL record the rejection with the User identifier, the Proposal identifier, and the decision timestamp, SHALL discard the Proposal without execution, and SHALL make no change to Tenant state for that Proposal.
11. WHEN THE FinanceOS_Action_Service completes execution of a Proposal, THE FinanceOS_Action_Service SHALL perform Verification within 60 seconds of execution completion by comparing the observed post-execution state against the Proposal expected outcome, treating a monetary difference of 1 paisa or less as matching.
12. IF Verification detects a monetary difference greater than 1 paisa or a non-monetary difference between observed state and the Proposal expected outcome, THEN THE FinanceOS_Action_Service SHALL mark the Proposal as verification-failed, SHALL create an Exception in the verification failure Exception_Category with the absolute INR difference as the INR impact and the Proposal identifier and target Source_Record identifiers attached, and SHALL make no further automatic change to Tenant state for that Proposal.
13. IF an executed Proposal or a Proposal awaiting approval with the same action type and the same target Source_Records exists for the Tenant in the Audit_Log within the preceding 30 days, THEN THE Policy_Engine SHALL return fail for the duplicate action Policy_Check and SHALL record the identifier of the matching Proposal.
14. FOR ALL Proposals reaching the EXECUTE stage, the Audit_Log SHALL contain an Authorization record referencing that Proposal.
15. WHEN an Agent submits a Proposal to THE Policy_Engine, THE Policy_Engine SHALL compute the Proposal risk score as an integer from 0 to 100 from the absolute INR impact of the Proposal, the Proposal action type, and the count of absent Evidence_Chain Source_Records, and SHALL compare that risk score against the Auto_Execute_Threshold, which is an integer from 0 to 100, defaults to 0, and is modifiable only by a User holding the Tenant policy configuration permission.
16. IF a Proposal holding the require-approval decision receives neither approval nor rejection within the Approval_Window, which is an integer from 1 hour to 168 hours and defaults to 24 hours, THEN THE FinanceOS_Action_Service SHALL mark the Proposal as expired, SHALL withhold execution permanently for that Proposal, and SHALL append an Audit_Event recording the expiry with the elapsed wait time.
17. IF execution of an authorized Proposal fails at the EXECUTE stage, THEN THE FinanceOS_Action_Service SHALL mark the Proposal as execution-failed, SHALL reverse each change already applied for that Proposal by creating a reversing Ledger_Entry set through THE Semantic_Ledger, SHALL create an Exception in the execution failure Exception_Category identifying the Proposal identifier and the failure reason, and SHALL attempt no further execution of that Proposal without a new Authorization.

### Requirement 12: Financial Tool Layer and Tool Grounding

**User Story:** As a finance operator, I want every number an agent states to be traceable to a source record, so that I can trust and verify AI output.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN an Agent reads Tenant financial data, THE Agent SHALL read the data through a Financial_Tool in THE Financial_Tool_Layer that declares a typed input schema and a typed output schema, and SHALL supply invocation arguments conforming to that typed input schema.
2. WHEN a Financial_Tool returns a monetary figure, THE Financial_Tool SHALL return the figure as an integer number of paise together with an Evidence_Chain containing every contributing Source_Record identifier with its Source_Record type, an ordered list of computation steps in which each step states one arithmetic operation and the Source_Record identifiers or preceding step outputs used as its operands, the total count of Source_Record identifiers in the Evidence_Chain, and the as-of timestamp of the most recently updated Source_Record in the Evidence_Chain, and SHALL return the Source_Record identifiers in retrievable pages of at most 500 identifiers per page without omitting any Source_Record identifier from the returned pages.
3. IF a Financial_Tool cannot read every Source_Record required to compose a complete Evidence_Chain for a monetary figure, THEN THE Financial_Tool SHALL return an incomplete-evidence result in place of the figure, SHALL omit the figure from its output, and SHALL identify each unavailable Source_Record type with the count of unavailable Source_Records of that type.
4. WHEN an Agent presents a monetary figure to a User, THE Agent SHALL present the figure with the identifier of its Evidence_Chain and the as-of timestamp of that Evidence_Chain, and SHALL re-invoke the Financial_Tool to obtain a new Evidence_Chain before presentation where the as-of timestamp is more than 15 minutes earlier than the presentation time.
5. WHEN a User selects a presented monetary figure, THE FinanceOS_UI SHALL display the Evidence_Chain computation steps in their stated order with the operation and operand references of each step, the Evidence_Chain as-of timestamp, the total count of Source_Record identifiers, and the Source_Record identifiers in pages of at most 100 identifiers per page with navigation to every remaining page, and SHALL indicate that the Evidence_Chain is stale where any referenced Source_Record has been updated after the Evidence_Chain as-of timestamp.
6. IF an Agent response contains a monetary figure with no Evidence_Chain identifier or with an Evidence_Chain identifier that does not resolve to a stored Evidence_Chain, THEN THE FinanceOS_Response_Validator SHALL withhold the entire response from the User, SHALL return an error indication stating that the figure is not Tool_Grounded, and SHALL append an Audit_Event to the Audit_Log recording the withheld response and the unresolved figure.
7. WHEN a Financial_Tool executes, THE Financial_Tool SHALL restrict its data reads and its data writes to the Tenant identifier of the requesting session, and SHALL perform no data write where the Financial_Tool is declared read-only.
8. FOR ALL monetary figures presented by an Agent, recomputing the figure by replaying the ordered Evidence_Chain computation steps over the referenced Source_Records as of the Evidence_Chain as-of timestamp SHALL produce a value equal to the presented figure in exact integer paise with zero difference.
9. IF an Agent invokes a Financial_Tool with an argument that does not conform to the declared typed input schema of that Financial_Tool, including any argument carrying free-form query text or free-form SQL, THEN THE Financial_Tool SHALL reject the invocation without reading Tenant data, SHALL return a schema-violation result identifying each non-conforming argument, and SHALL append an Audit_Event recording the rejected invocation.
10. WHERE a Financial_Tool is declared write-capable, IF the invocation carries no reference to a Proposal holding a recorded Authorization, THEN THE Financial_Tool SHALL reject the invocation, SHALL leave Tenant state unchanged, and SHALL append an Audit_Event recording the rejected invocation.
11. IF a Financial_Tool does not return a result within 10 seconds of invocation or returns an execution error, THEN THE Financial_Tool_Layer SHALL terminate the invocation, SHALL leave Tenant state unchanged, and SHALL return a tool-failure result to the invoking Agent identifying the Financial_Tool and the failure cause of timeout or execution error.

### Requirement 13: Audit Log Immutability

**User Story:** As a business owner, I want an immutable record of every AI detection, decision, and action, so that I can prove what happened and why.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN THE FinanceOS_Audit_Service appends an Audit_Event, THE FinanceOS_Audit_Service SHALL store the event type, the Tenant identifier, a Tenant-scoped sequence number equal to 1 for the first Audit_Event of that Tenant and equal to the preceding Audit_Event sequence number plus 1 thereafter, an actor identifier that is exactly one of a User identifier, an Agent name, or the Policy_Engine identifier, the Proposal identifier where the Audit_Event relates to a Proposal, the affected Source_Record identifiers, a timestamp expressed in UTC to millisecond precision, and an event payload.
2. WHEN THE FinanceOS_Audit_Service appends an Audit_Event, THE FinanceOS_Audit_Service SHALL exclude every credential value from the event payload and SHALL reference affected Source_Records by identifier rather than storing a copy of the Source_Record field content in the event payload.
3. IF the event payload supplied for an Audit_Event exceeds 65536 bytes, THEN THE FinanceOS_Audit_Service SHALL append the Audit_Event with the payload reduced to at most 65536 bytes, SHALL store an indicator that the payload was reduced, and SHALL store the affected Source_Record identifiers unreduced.
4. WHEN THE FinanceOS_Audit_Service appends an Audit_Event, THE FinanceOS_Audit_Service SHALL store a Chain_Value computed over that Audit_Event's stored fields together with the Chain_Value of the Audit_Event holding the immediately preceding sequence number for the same Tenant, and SHALL use a fixed initial Chain_Value for the Audit_Event with sequence number 1.
5. IF a request attempts to update or delete an existing Audit_Event, THEN THE FinanceOS_Audit_Service SHALL reject the request with an error indicating that the Audit_Log is append-only, SHALL leave the targeted Audit_Event's sequence number, timestamp, actor identifier, event payload, and Chain_Value unchanged, and SHALL append an Audit_Event recording the rejected attempt with the requesting actor identifier and the targeted sequence number.
6. WHEN a User requests the history of a Source_Record, THE FinanceOS_Audit_Service SHALL return the Audit_Events referencing that Source_Record ordered by ascending timestamp with equal timestamps ordered by ascending Tenant-scoped sequence number, in pages of at most 100 Audit_Events, and SHALL return with each page an indicator of whether further Audit_Events remain.
7. WHEN a User requests the history of a Proposal, THE FinanceOS_Audit_Service SHALL return exactly 1 Audit_Event per completed Action_Pipeline stage for that Proposal ordered by ascending Tenant-scoped sequence number, and SHALL identify each of the 7 Action_Pipeline stages for which no Audit_Event exists as not completed.
8. WHEN a User requests verification of the Audit_Log for a Tenant, THE FinanceOS_Audit_Service SHALL recompute the Chain_Value of each Audit_Event in ascending sequence number order and SHALL return a verification result stating either that every recomputed Chain_Value equals its stored Chain_Value and no sequence number is absent, or the lowest sequence number whose recomputed Chain_Value differs from its stored Chain_Value and the lowest absent sequence number.
9. THE FinanceOS_Audit_Service SHALL retain every appended Audit_Event readable and unchanged for at least the Tenant-configured retention period, and the Tenant-configured retention period SHALL be no shorter than 2555 days measured from the Audit_Event timestamp.
10. FOR ALL Audit_Events appended for a Tenant, reading the Audit_Log at a later time SHALL return each previously appended Audit_Event with the same sequence number, the same timestamp, the same actor identifier, the same event payload, and the same Chain_Value.

### Requirement 14: Multi-Tenancy and Security

**User Story:** As a platform operator, I want strict tenant isolation enforced at the data layer, so that no tenant can read or modify another tenant's financial data.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN THE FinanceOS_Data_Layer stores a record containing Tenant financial data, THE FinanceOS_Data_Layer SHALL store exactly 1 non-empty Tenant identifier on that record.
2. WHEN an authenticated session issues a read or a write against Tenant financial data, THE FinanceOS_Data_Layer SHALL evaluate a row-level security policy bound to the session's Tenant identifier before any row is returned or persisted, and SHALL apply that policy independently of any Tenant filter supplied by application code.
3. IF an authenticated session requests or attempts to modify a record whose Tenant identifier differs from the session's Tenant identifier, THEN THE FinanceOS_Data_Layer SHALL return zero rows, SHALL leave the stored record unchanged, and SHALL append an Audit_Event recording the User identifier, the session's Tenant identifier, the requested record type, the requested record identifier, and the timestamp.
4. IF a request targeting Tenant financial data carries no session credential, an expired session credential, or an invalid session credential, THEN THE FinanceOS_API SHALL return an authentication-required error and SHALL return no Tenant financial data and no Tenant identifier.
5. WHEN THE FinanceOS_Configuration_Service stores or replaces a Razorpay API credential or a Model_Provider API credential, THE FinanceOS_Configuration_Service SHALL store the credential value in encrypted form, SHALL exclude the credential value from API responses, log output, and error messages, SHALL return only a masked reference in place of the credential value for every subsequent read request, and SHALL append an Audit_Event recording the store or replace event without the credential value.
6. WHEN a User initiates an action requiring one of the Permissions view financial data, run agents, approve sensitive actions, configure policy, manage credentials, or manage users, THE FinanceOS_Authorization_Service SHALL verify that the User holds that Permission within the session's Tenant identifier before the action reads or changes any Tenant financial data.
7. FOR ALL queries executed against Tenant financial data, the count of returned rows whose Tenant identifier differs from the Tenant identifier scope of the executing caller SHALL equal 0.
8. WHEN a User authenticates, THE FinanceOS_API SHALL bind the session to exactly 1 Tenant identifier selected from the Tenants in which the User holds membership, SHALL keep that bound Tenant identifier unchanged for the lifetime of the session, and SHALL require establishment of a new session before the User acts within a different Tenant.
9. IF THE FinanceOS_Authorization_Service determines that the User does not hold the Permission required for a requested action within the session's Tenant, THEN THE FinanceOS_Authorization_Service SHALL deny the action, SHALL make no change to Tenant state, SHALL return a permission-denied error identifying the required Permission, and SHALL append an Audit_Event recording the User identifier, the session's Tenant identifier, the required Permission, the requested action type, and the timestamp.
10. IF a server-side privileged access path issues a read or a write against Tenant financial data without an explicit Tenant identifier scope, THEN THE FinanceOS_Data_Layer SHALL reject the request, SHALL return no Tenant financial data, SHALL leave stored records unchanged, and SHALL append an Audit_Event recording the rejected request and the timestamp.

### Requirement 15: Accuracy and Performance Bounds

**User Story:** As a finance operator, I want reconciliation results to be arithmetically exact and returned within a workable time, so that I can rely on FinanceOS during a working session.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN THE FinanceOS_Calculation_Service performs a monetary computation, THE FinanceOS_Calculation_Service SHALL compute every operand, intermediate value, and result as an integer number of paise within the range -99999999999999 paise to 99999999999999 paise, and SHALL exclude floating-point representation of monetary values from the computation.
2. WHEN THE FinanceOS_Calculation_Service converts a paise value to a displayed INR value, THE FinanceOS_Calculation_Service SHALL round half up to 2 decimal places and SHALL leave the stored integer paise value unchanged.
3. WHILE no more than 5 Agent runs are executing concurrently for the Tenant, WHEN THE Reconciliation_Agent runs over a Tenant dataset of up to 5000 Payments, THE Reconciliation_Agent SHALL return the complete Exception set within 60 seconds measured from run start to results available.
4. WHILE no more than 5 Agent runs are executing concurrently for the Tenant, WHEN a User submits a question to an Agent, THE Agent SHALL return the first displayable content of the response within 15 seconds measured from submission, and SHALL return the complete answer within 120 seconds measured from submission.
5. WHILE no more than 5 Agent runs are executing concurrently for the Tenant, WHEN a User opens THE Control_Tower for a Tenant whose metric computation is complete, THE Control_Tower SHALL display all 4 top metrics within 3 seconds measured from the User request to the metrics being rendered.
6. IF an Agent run reaches 120 seconds without completing, THEN THE Agent SHALL stop the run, SHALL return the partial results computed so far, SHALL identify the run as incomplete, and SHALL identify which Source_Record types were not fully processed.
7. FOR ALL reconciliation runs over an unchanged Tenant dataset, repeating the run SHALL produce the same Exception set in the same order, the same INR impact value per Exception, and the same Source_Record identifier references per Exception.
8. THE FinanceOS_Data_Layer SHALL store every monetary value as an integer number of paise within the range -99999999999999 paise to 99999999999999 paise and SHALL exclude floating-point representation of monetary values.
9. WHEN THE FinanceOS_Calculation_Service applies a percentage or rate multiplication to a paise value and the exact product is not a whole number of paise, THE FinanceOS_Calculation_Service SHALL round the product half up to the nearest whole paisa and SHALL report the rounding adjustment in paise with the result.
10. IF a Tenant dataset contains more than 5000 Payments, THEN THE Reconciliation_Agent SHALL process every Payment in the dataset, SHALL report the count of Payments processed, and SHALL identify that the 60-second bound does not apply to the run.

### Requirement 17: Secret-Safe Model Provider Runtime Verification

**User Story:** As a platform operator, I want secret-safe runtime readiness and routing verification for every Model_Provider, so that I can diagnose provider configuration and failover without exposing credentials or Tenant financial data.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE FinanceOS_Configuration_Service SHALL resolve each Gemini, Groq, and OpenRouter credential exclusively from the Server_Runtime_Environment or Encrypted_Secret_Storage and SHALL expose only a Masked_Credential_Reference outside authorized server processes.
2. IF a Gemini, Groq, or OpenRouter credential value is detected in a source file, client bundle, log record, Model prompt, error message, test fixture, or Audit_Event payload, THEN THE Secret_Safety_Gate SHALL block the associated build, release, request, or write before the affected artifact is distributed or persisted.
3. WHEN a User holding the manage credentials Permission initiates provider readiness verification, THE Provider_Runtime_Verifier SHALL execute exactly 1 Provider_Readiness_Check for Gemini, exactly 1 Provider_Readiness_Check for Groq, and exactly 1 Provider_Readiness_Check for OpenRouter.
4. WHEN THE Provider_Runtime_Verifier assembles a Provider_Readiness_Check or Routing_Verification request, THE Provider_Runtime_Verifier SHALL use a Non_Financial_Probe containing 0 Tenant identifiers, 0 Source_Record fields, 0 monetary figures, 0 Tenant-derived text, and 0 credential values.
5. IF THE FinanceOS_Configuration_Service resolves no credential for the Model_Provider under check, THEN THE Provider_Runtime_Verifier SHALL produce a Provider_Readiness_Result with the Provider_Diagnostic_Outcome missing credential, SHALL use the unavailable marker for the resolved Model name, and SHALL send 0 requests to that Model_Provider.
6. IF a Model_Provider rejects a Non_Financial_Probe because the supplied credential is not accepted, THEN THE Provider_Runtime_Verifier SHALL produce a Provider_Readiness_Result with the Provider_Diagnostic_Outcome invalid credential and SHALL exclude the credential value and provider response body from the result.
7. IF a Model_Provider does not return a complete Non_Financial_Probe response within the request timeout defined in Requirement 11.5, THEN THE Provider_Runtime_Verifier SHALL produce a Provider_Readiness_Result with the Provider_Diagnostic_Outcome timeout.
8. IF a Model_Provider returns a rate limit response to a Non_Financial_Probe, THEN THE Provider_Runtime_Verifier SHALL produce a Provider_Readiness_Result with the Provider_Diagnostic_Outcome rate limit.
9. IF a Model_Provider returns a non-success response that is not a credential rejection, timeout, or rate limit response, THEN THE Provider_Runtime_Verifier SHALL produce a Provider_Readiness_Result with the Provider_Diagnostic_Outcome provider failure.
10. WHEN a Model_Provider returns a complete successful response to a Non_Financial_Probe, THE Provider_Runtime_Verifier SHALL produce a Provider_Readiness_Result with the Provider_Diagnostic_Outcome ready and SHALL record the resolved Model name returned or selected for that request.
11. WHEN a Provider_Readiness_Check completes, THE Provider_Runtime_Verifier SHALL record one Provider_Readiness_Result containing the Model_Provider name, the resolved Model name or unavailable marker, elapsed latency as an integer number of milliseconds at or above 0, exactly one Provider_Diagnostic_Outcome, a redacted diagnostic code containing no credential characters and no provider response body, and the check timestamp in UTC to millisecond precision.
12. WHERE the declared Task_Class is complex reasoning, WHEN THE Provider_Runtime_Verifier executes a Routing_Verification with all Model_Providers available, THE Provider_Runtime_Verifier SHALL mark first-provider routing as passed only where the actual first Model_Provider attempt is OpenRouter.
13. WHERE the declared Task_Class is document analysis, WHEN THE Provider_Runtime_Verifier executes a Routing_Verification with all Model_Providers available, THE Provider_Runtime_Verifier SHALL mark first-provider routing as passed only where the actual first Model_Provider attempt is Gemini.
14. WHERE the declared Task_Class is fast classification, WHEN THE Provider_Runtime_Verifier executes a Routing_Verification with all Model_Providers available, THE Provider_Runtime_Verifier SHALL mark first-provider routing as passed only where the actual first Model_Provider attempt is Groq.
15. WHEN a Routing_Verification applies a controlled timeout or rate limit outcome to the intended first Model_Provider, THE Provider_Runtime_Verifier SHALL record the initial attempt and each retry, SHALL verify that the first Model_Provider receives at most 2 retry attempts, and SHALL mark fallback routing as passed only where the actual next Model_Provider is Gemini for complex reasoning, OpenRouter for document analysis, or Gemini for fast classification.
16. WHEN a Routing_Verification applies a controlled provider failure outcome to the intended first Model_Provider, THE Provider_Runtime_Verifier SHALL verify that the first Model_Provider receives 0 retry attempts and SHALL mark fallback routing as passed only where the actual next Model_Provider is Gemini for complex reasoning, OpenRouter for document analysis, or Gemini for fast classification.
17. WHEN a Routing_Verification completes, THE Provider_Runtime_Verifier SHALL record the declared Task_Class, the expected first Model_Provider, the ordered actual Model_Provider attempts, the retry count per attempted Model_Provider, the expected next Model_Provider, the actual next Model_Provider or unavailable marker, a pass or fail result, and the verification timestamp in UTC to millisecond precision.
18. WHEN a User holding the manage credentials Permission requests provider readiness information, THE FinanceOS_UI SHALL display the latest Provider_Readiness_Result for Gemini, Groq, and OpenRouter and the latest Routing_Verification result for each Task_Class without displaying a credential value, a provider authorization header, a provider request body, or a provider response body.
